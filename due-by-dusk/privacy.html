Privacy policy · updated 27 October 2026

Your farm stays on your phone.

Due by Dusk is a farming game from Khoreh. It needs no account, and your farm is saved on your own device. The only part that talks to anyone else is the optional ads. Here is all of it, in plain words.

In short: no accounts, no analytics, no in-app purchases. Your save stays on your device. Ads are optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob: you decide when to watch one for an in-game bonus, and Google receives some device information to show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud.

What stays on your device

Your farm and progress (bills, equipment, perks, heirlooms, skills and the map), a few backup copies of your save, and your settings (finger offset, vibration, reduced effects, and whether you finished the tutorial) are kept in the game's storage on your phone. They are not sent to us.

If you use Export save, the game makes a save file and you decide where it goes. Import save reads only the file or text you choose. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this from your phone. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the game's data in your device backup to your Google account, under Google's terms.

Apart from ads, the game makes no network requests: its art and fonts are packed inside the app.

Ads from Google AdMob

The game shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. They are optional: the game offers a bonus, and an ad plays only if you choose to watch it. While the game runs, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • how you use the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and ad video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google explains how it uses it in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We don't receive this information about you: AdMob only gives us totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before your data is used for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time under Privacy choices in the game's Management panel.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (often under Privacy, then Ads).
  • Uninstall. Removing the game stops all of the above.

Children

Due by Dusk is not made for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 is playing, tell us at the address below and we will help.
