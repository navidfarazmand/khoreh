Privacy policy · updated 22 November 2026 (version 0.6.3)

Your yarn stays on your phone.

Tapestry is a yarn puzzle game for Android from Khoreh. It needs no account, and your progress is saved on your own device. The parts that talk to anyone else are the ads, anonymous game statistics, the update check and, only if you send one, a bug report. Here is all of it, in plain words.

In short: no accounts, no real-money purchases. Your progress stays on your device. Ads come from Google AdMob: most are rewarded videos you decide to watch for extra coins, an extra slot, a heart or a booster, and from level 8 a short ad also plays between some levels. Google receives some device information to show the ads, measure them and prevent fraud. From version 0.6.3 the game also sends anonymous statistics about how it is played to Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can see which levels are too hard or too easy; they carry no name, email or account. A bug report reaches us only if you write one and tap Send.

What stays on your device

Your level, coins, boosters, hearts, daily gift progress and sound settings are kept in a small file in the game's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the game's data in your device backup to your Google account, under Google's terms.

Apart from ads, game statistics, the update check and bug reports you choose to send, the game makes no network requests of its own: its art, music and fonts are packed inside the app.

Ads from Google AdMob

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Most are rewarded ads: the game offers a bonus, and the ad plays only if you choose to watch it. From level 8, a short ad also plays between some levels. While the game runs, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • how you use the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and ad video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google explains how it uses it in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We don't receive this information about you: AdMob only gives us totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

From version 0.6.3, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how it is played, so we can balance the levels and fix what goes wrong. Google collects, and we see in Firebase:

  • a random ID for this installation of the game (not your name, email or Google account);
  • your device's IP address, from which Google estimates your country, region and city; the address itself is not shown to us;
  • device and app details: phone model, Android version, the game's version and your language;
  • how you play: the levels you start, win or lose and how long they take, the boosters, coins and hearts you use, the daily gift, the daily challenge and Granny's knit, the videos you choose to watch and the ads between levels (where in the game, and how many), when the game opens, how long you play and when it goes to the background.

We see this per installation and in totals; we do not know who you are. Google keeps it for us for up to 14 months and processes it under the Firebase data processing terms and the Google Privacy Policy. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game tells Analytics not to use this data for advertising.

Bug reports

You can report a problem from inside the game: shake your phone hard, or open Settings and tap Report a problem. A form opens where you write what went wrong. It shows a picture of the game screen, which you can leave out with the box next to it, and says what else is sent. Nothing leaves your phone unless you tap Send.

When you tap Send, the game sends us your message; the picture, if you kept it; the game version, your level, coins and hearts; your phone model, Android version, screen size, memory and language; a random number the game made on your phone, so we can tell several reports from one phone apart; and the game's recent log messages. The report goes through a small Google Apps Script web app in Khoreh's Google account, which emails it to us at bugs@khoreh.com, so Google processes it for us. We use reports only to find and fix problems, never for ads, and delete them once the problem is solved.

Update check

When the game starts, it asks the Google Play app on your phone whether a newer version of Tapestry is out, and if there is one, it offers to update. This happens between your phone and Google Play; we receive nothing from it.

Your choices

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before your data is used for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time under Privacy choices in the game's Settings.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (often under Privacy, then Ads).
  • Statistics. To have the statistics from your phone deleted, write to us at the address below; uninstalling the game ends them, and Google deletes them within 14 months.
  • Uninstall. Removing the game stops all of the above.

Children

Tapestry is not made for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 is playing, tell us at the address below and we will help.
