Privacy policy · updated 16 November 2026

Your yarn stays on your phone.

Tapestry is a yarn puzzle game for Android from Khoreh. It needs no account, and your progress is saved on your own device. The only part that talks to anyone else is the optional ads. Here is all of it, in plain words.

In short: no accounts, no analytics, no real-money purchases. Your progress stays on your device. Ads are optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob: you decide when to watch one for extra coins, an extra slot or a booster, and Google receives some device information to show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud.

What stays on your device

Your level, coins, boosters, daily gift progress and sound settings are kept in a small file in the game's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the game's data in your device backup to your Google account, under Google's terms.

Apart from ads, the game makes no network requests of its own: its art, music and fonts are packed inside the app.

Ads from Google AdMob

The game shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. They are optional: the game offers a bonus, and an ad plays only if you choose to watch it. While the game runs, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • how you use the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and ad video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google explains how it uses it in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We don't receive this information about you: AdMob only gives us totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before your data is used for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time under Privacy choices in the game's Settings.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (often under Privacy, then Ads).
  • Uninstall. Removing the game stops all of the above.

Children

Tapestry is not made for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 is playing, tell us at the address below and we will help.
